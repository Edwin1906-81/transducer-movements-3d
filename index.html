Terms follow the ASE guideline for performing a comprehensive transthoracic echocardiographic examination in adults (Mitchell C, et al. J Am Soc Echocardiogr. 2019;32:1-64), which defines slide, rock, tilt, and rotate, plus angle and sweep. "Fanning" in point-of-care teaching corresponds to ASE tilting.
The green dot on the probe is the orientation indicator; its clock position is shown with the patient's head at 12 o'clock and the patient's left at 3 o'clock. The echo image follows the cardiology convention, with the indicator side on screen right. Anatomy is simplified for teaching.
